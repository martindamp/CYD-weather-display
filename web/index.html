CYD Weather — Web installer

Current weather and a 5-day forecast for your favourite places on a Cheap Yellow Display. Install it straight from the browser — no Arduino IDE or PlatformIO needed.

Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (not mobile).
How to
  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Plug the CYD into USB.
  3. Click Install CYD Weather and pick the serial port (CH340/CP2102).
  4. When the install is done, the installer asks for your Wi-Fi — pick the network and enter the password.
  5. Tap + Add location on the screen, start typing a city and tap one of the suggestions.
  6. Add more locations from the menu (☰ top right). With more than one, the pages rotate automatically — tap left/right to flip manually.

Tip: the device also has its own web page at http://<device-ip>/ (the IP is shown on the screen and in the menu), where you can add, reorder and remove locations from your phone, change the rotation speed and update the firmware. Wi-Fi can be changed any time by reconnecting here and choosing Connect to Wi-Fi.
